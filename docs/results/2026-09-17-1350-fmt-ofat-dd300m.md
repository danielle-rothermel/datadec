# One-factor format effects, DataDecide 300M, SNR train and dev subsets (2026-09-17 13:50)

First clean format run: canonical OLMES prompt with no instruction, one axis changed at a time
(axes 1 separator, 2 descriptor pair incl. two random-string descriptors, 5 text-to-option
separator (MC only), 6 shot count), 300 items each on the SNR train and dev subsets, RC and
MC. Sweeps: `~/drotherm/data/runs/po/clean-20260917/sweeps/fmt-ofat-1256-dd300m-{train,dev}`;
pairs file and per-format effects table copied to `assets/2026-09-17-fmt-ofat-dd300m/`.
Effects are paired per item against canonical; se is the paired standard error (n=300).

## Summary

- **Two formats hurt RC clearly and replicate on dev:** the random-phrase descriptor
  ("View Mobile Site:" in place of "Answer:") costs about 0.10 accuracy and 0.014 on the
  per-char share, and zero shots costs about 0.07 accuracy and 0.009 on the share. One shot
  costs 0.003 on the share on both subsets while its accuracy effect flips sign, which is
  the likelihood metric resolving what accuracy cannot at n=300.
- **Two small RC effects replicate at 2-3 se:** `Input/Output` descriptors (-0.0017) and the
  colon-newline separator (-0.0022). `Q/A`, the dash separator, and the random vocabulary
  string ("ceil Ai aggress:") are indistinguishable from canonical on both subsets.
- **The two random descriptors disagree**, which is the interesting part: a nonsense
  vocabulary string is as good as "Answer:", while a fluent but wrong phrase is the worst
  format tested. For a 300M model the descriptor slot is not inert, but what hurts is a
  misleading natural-language cue, not the absence of a meaningful one. Lu 2023's
  "random strings are competitive" holds for the vocabulary string here and fails for the
  phrase.
- **MC stays at chance for every format.** The largest MC movements are the random-phrase
  descriptor (+0.04 / +0.07 accuracy on train / dev, share +0.003 / +0.005, about 1 se) and
  zero shots (+0.05 on train, +0.01 on dev). Nothing clears 2 se on the share; the
  text-to-option separator does nothing. Whether some format lifts DD off chance on MC
  remains open but this pass found no candidate.
- **Paired standard errors on the RC share are 0.0003-0.002**, several times smaller than the
  0.0017 implied by the unconditional item sd, because most items move little under a
  format change. Format main effects of 0.001 are resolvable at n=300 on this model.

- **Format effects are model-specific, in sign as well as size.** `Q/A` is neutral on 300M
  and a replicated loss on 1B; `Input/Output` is a loss on 300M and a small gain on 1B; the
  dash separator is neutral on 300M and a replicated gain on 1B. The random-phrase
  descriptor that cost 300M 0.014 costs 1B only 0.003, while the random vocabulary string
  that 300M ignored costs 1B 0.0035. The only effect shared by both models is zero shots at
  about −0.008. This is the per-model selection rule of SAMMO and Bozhenko observed
  directly at 300M vs 1B.
- **Colon-newline splits the two metrics on 1B.** Accuracy drops 12-15 points on both
  subsets, the largest effect in the run, while the per-char share moves only −0.005 at
  1.5 se. The share is compressed toward chance and the ranking flips concentrate on items
  where the share barely moves, so here accuracy is the more sensitive readout. The 300M
  version of the same format lost 2 points of accuracy. Worth a look at the tokenisation of
  "Answer:\n" plus a space-prefixed continuation on the 1B tokenizer.
- **MC stays at chance on 1B for every format**, as on 300M; no share effect exceeds 1.5 se.

## Caveats

- Two models so far; the Qwen replicates of this design are running.
- The two random descriptors are single draws; the difference between them is a
  difference between two strings, not between two generation methods. More draws would
  be needed to say which kind of random string hurts.
- MC scoring is the current bare-label rule; the surface-form set is not yet implemented.
- Shot-count changes also change prompt length, so the 0-shot effect bundles "no
  demonstrations" with "no example separator above the query".
